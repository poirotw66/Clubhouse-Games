Field guide & tactical rules

Your turn: 2 AP. Move, attack, skill, pickup, or use a potion for 1 AP. AP reaching zero automatically resolves all enemy actions.

Move: Select Aria, then a cyan tile. Movement follows a deterministic shortest walkable path, up to 3 orthogonal steps. Dash allows 4. Walls, pillars, rubble, and enemies block paths. Crossing spikes inflicts 20 damage.

Combat: Ranges use Manhattan distance. Magical projectiles ignore cover; there is no line-of-sight system. Basic Attack deals 18. Enemy attacks are reduced by Aria’s 5 defense, to a minimum of 1.

Information: Select an enemy and inspect it for free to reveal exact stats. Map and tile inspection are free. Everything the agent knows is also available to the human.

Resources: Heal restores 30 HP. Potions restore 35 HP or 25 mana. Barrier halves the next incoming attack after defense, rounded up; it expires at the end of the next enemy phase after this turn.

Cooldowns: Arcane Shot is usable again 2 turn numbers after casting; Heal after 3. Potions do not have cooldowns. Orcs take 10 bonus damage from Arcane Shot.

Enemies: Each enemy either attacks or moves per enemy phase, never both. Goblins and skeletons activate within 5 tiles; the guardian activates within 3 tiles of the crystal or when Aria is adjacent.

Escape: Collect the crystal, stand on the portal, then Leave. Tutorial is one floor. Campaign/Daily have 6 floors; clearing a floor offers a three-choice reward before descending. Cover tiles halve ranged hits against Aria.

Shortcuts: 1 Attack · 2 Arcane Shot · 3 Dash · 4 Heal · 5 Barrier. Arrow keys or WASD move one tile. Tab and Enter work on every tile and control.

How WebMCP powers this dungeon

No backend · No DOM guessing
  1. The game exposes structured tactical capabilities.
  2. The AI agent discovers these capabilities through WebMCP.
  3. The agent observes the battlefield through structured tools.
  4. The agent reasons about enemies, resources, and objectives.
  5. The agent chooses an individual tactical action.
  6. GameEngine validates and executes that action.
  7. The battlefield immediately reflects the same shared state.
  8. The agent observes again, adapts, and continues.
