01 / The exhibition

What happens when sculpture
refuses to be still?

A counterweight shifts. A motor turns. A breath of air changes everything. Nothing Stands Still explores sculpture not as a fixed object, but as an unfolding event—shaped by gravity, time, and your presence.

Across three galleries, artists turn simple mechanical principles into questions about control and chance. Some works repeat with machine-like precision. Others never make quite the same movement twice. Look longer. The work is still becoming.

A Measure of Air

Balanced at the edge of movement, Voss’s sculptures register the slightest disturbance. Even the air displaced by your arrival becomes part of the work.

Almost, Again

Ivo Renn · Brass, motor, variable time

2025 ↗

Four rings rotate at subtly different speeds. Alignments emerge, hold for a moment, then disappear. Repetition becomes a way of noticing difference.
