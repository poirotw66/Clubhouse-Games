A FIELD GUIDE TO THE IMPOSSIBLE

Build your own peril.

01 · Walk without orders.
The traveller follows the road and fights automatically. Camp restores 40% HP and refills draughts each circuit.
02 · Remember the world.
Select or drag a card onto a highlighted cell. Road cards occupy the path; verge cards must touch it. Every card has a price.
03 · Wear what survives.
Click recovered equipment to replace a relic. Hover to compare. Arrows compare total weighted power, not your build.
04 · Listen for the bell.
Twenty placements awaken the Ninth Bell at the southeast seal. Its toll strikes through armor every six seconds.
05 · Know when to leave.
Camp retreat: 100% resources. Elsewhere: 60%. Death: 25%. You may arrange an automatic return at the next camp.
